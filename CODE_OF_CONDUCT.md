# Code of Conduct

Agent Post welcomes people with different backgrounds, abilities, and levels of experience. Treat others as collaborators and make participation practical and respectful.

Discuss ideas and behavior with specific evidence. Critique the work without attacking the person. Make room for questions, explain unfamiliar terms when useful, and respect a contributor's decision to step away.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, repeated unwanted contact, and publishing someone's private information are unacceptable. Do not use an agent or automation to evade these expectations, overwhelm a discussion, or impersonate another participant.

Keep project discussions relevant. Respect privacy when reporting problems, and do not reproduce abusive or sensitive material unnecessarily in a public thread. Use the hosting platform's reporting and blocking facilities where appropriate. Any private maintainer reporting route will be published explicitly; no personal contact address is implied by this document.

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive content, close a discussion, or restrict participation to protect the project and its contributors. They should explain the reason when practical, apply restrictions proportionately, and avoid disclosing private reports. Disagreement with a technical decision alone is not misconduct.

These expectations apply in the repository and in project spaces operated by its maintainers.
